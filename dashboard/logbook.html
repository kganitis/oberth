Session time adds up the turns of your assistant's sessions, leaving out any silence over 5 minutes, so a single step that ran longer counts for less. It shows where sessions went, not hours at the keyboard. A turn counts on the day it started. The cost is what the turns' tokens (subagents' included) would cost at the API's prices: an estimate of the work's size, not a bill, since a subscription charges none of it per token.
