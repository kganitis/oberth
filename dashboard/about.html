Dev Project Console

Every project in your project folders on one local page and in the menu bar. Oberth shows what is running, starts and stops dev servers, and picks a project back up in your editor or your AI assistant.

The name

Hermann Oberth was a pioneer of rocketry, and the Oberth effect carries his name. A rocket gets the most from its fuel when it fires at its fastest, at the deepest point of an orbit, so a small burn there goes a long way.

The emblem

The O sits off-centre in its ring, thinnest at the green dot, the way an orbit has one lowest point. The dot is a jewel pilot lamp, the lit lens on a control panel that means something is running. Here the O is vermilion enamel with a gold lattice on a midnight dial, and the lamp sits in a gold hex nut.
